Sierra Club Griffith Park Thursday Evening Hikes

Thu · 2026-12-10 · 19:00 — Merry Go Round Lot Two, Los Angeles — Free

For a guided evening workout on varied trails and a chance to meet local hikers.

Free evening hikes meet at Merry Go Round Lot Two in Griffith Park at 7:00pm, offering simultaneous, leader-led routes across a range of difficulty levels (roughly level 1–6) and lasting about two hours; events typically draw 30–60 people. Newcomers should arrive early (~6:40pm) to sign the required waiver (pink waiver card valid through end of 2026), stick to level 1–3 if new, and bring water and a light jacket.
