DUBLAB presents: COMMONTHREAD with Eva Noxious

Sat · 2026-12-19 · 21:00 — The Airliner, Los Angeles — $20.00

A cross-genre bill that blends classic hip‑hop and G‑funk with adventurous house, techno and crate-digging vinyl sets.

The bill pairs Eva Noxious's independent hip-hop—drawing on classic hip-hop and G-funk—with Santiago Salazar's expansive, soulful DJ sets that move through Latin house, hi‑tech jazz, Detroit techno, electro and ghetto tech. Also featured are three6sashia, an Oakland vinyl DJ who folds jazz and international sounds into groove-driven sets, and LA-based producer/DJ Yessi, whose practice spans production, composition and sound design.
