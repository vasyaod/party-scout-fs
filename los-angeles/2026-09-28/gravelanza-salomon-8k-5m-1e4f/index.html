Salomon's gravel-running festival hits Griffith Park with a 5-mile solo race, a 6-person Run Crew Battle relay, and a live-music festival hub

Salomon's Gravelanza tour lands in Griffith Park with two ways to run: a 5-mile solo race (864ft climbing, 9am start) and a 6-person Run Crew Battle relay over 1.6-mile laps (11am, finals at noon). Live music runs start to finish with a headline set at the post-race celebration, plus a festival hub with a Salomon shoe-charms bar, tattoo artists, and food and drink.
