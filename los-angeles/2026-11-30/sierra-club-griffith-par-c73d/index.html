Sierra Club Griffith Park Tuesday Evening Hikes

Tue · 2026-12-01 · 19:00 — Merry Go Round Lot Two, Los Angeles — Free

Get an evening outdoor workout and meet fellow hikers across a range of abilities.

Tuesday evening conditioning hikes in Griffith Park start at 7:00 PM from the Merry Go Round Lot Two; simultaneous leader-led routes cover levels 1–6, last about two hours, and typically draw 30–60 people. Newcomers should arrive early to sign a waiver (pink waiver card issued if needed) and bring water and appropriate footwear.
