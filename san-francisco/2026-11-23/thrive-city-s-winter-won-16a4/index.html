Thrive City’s “Winter Wonderland” 2026 Tree Lighting Ceremony (SF)

Fri · 2026-11-27 · 16:00 — Thrive City, San Francisco — Free

The centerpiece is the lighting of the Bay Area’s 90‑foot holiday tree, paired with live soulful sets and special guest appearances.

Soulful performances from Oakland icons Goapele and The California Honeydrops anchor an evening of live routines from local dance troupes and the Hardwood Classics, plus special guest appearances from the Golden State Warriors and Valkyries. Festive extras include a Santa Photo Station, holiday arts & crafts, seasonal treats and a countdown to light the Bay Area’s 90‑foot holiday tree under falling snow.
