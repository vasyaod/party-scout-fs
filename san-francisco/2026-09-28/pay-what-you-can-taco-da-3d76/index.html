A pay-what-you-can taco plate that funds a free meal for someone else with every full payment

Every Friday, Tato's Bayview taqueria lets customers pay anywhere from $0 to $10 for a two-taco plate with sides, and when someone pays the full $10 the shop donates a free meal to someone in need in the city.
