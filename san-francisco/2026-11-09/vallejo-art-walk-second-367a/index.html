The monthly Vallejo Art Walk showcases a variety of nationally accomplished and upcoming local creatives in the historic downtown Vallejo arts district from 5–10 pm on the second Friday of every month. It spans Georgia, Marin, and Virginia streets with over 25 participating galleries and shops and Art Window displays.
