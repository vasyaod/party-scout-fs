Muir Woods Canopy Trail Loop Hiking

Sat · 2026-11-14 · 10:00 — Safeway, 2020 Market St, San Francisco, CA 94114, USA, SF — Free

To enjoy a short redwood canopy hike with creek views and convenient carpooling.

Meet at Safeway (2020 Market St) at 10:00 for a group trip to Muir Woods to hike the Canopy View and Fern Creek Loop — a moderate 3.1–3.8 mile route with about 580–820 ft of elevation gain, taking roughly 1.5–2.5 hours. Carpool signups are handled via the event WhatsApp group and attendees split gas/vehicle costs.
