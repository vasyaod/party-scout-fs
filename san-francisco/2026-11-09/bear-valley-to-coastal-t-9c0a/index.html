Bear Valley to Coastal Trail Hiking

Wed · 2026-11-11 · 08:00 — Safeway, 2020 Market St, San Francisco, CA 94114, USA, SF — Free

To spend a half-day on a scenic, moderate coastal hike from forest to ocean bluffs.

A free group hike meeting at Safeway (2020 Market St) that goes to Point Reyes National Seashore, following the Bear Valley to Coastal Trail route. The route is about 9.2 miles round-trip, moderate difficulty with ~738 ft elevation gain, and takes roughly 3.5–4 hours; carpool sign-up is coordinated via a WhatsApp group and vehicle costs are split if applicable.
