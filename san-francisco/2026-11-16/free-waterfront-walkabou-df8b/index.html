Thu · 2026-11-19 · 17:00 — The Embarcadero (btwn Pier 14 & the Exploratorium), San Francisco — Free

To enjoy a free, family-friendly waterfront evening of public art, guided tours, live music, and seasonal activities.

Waterfront Walkabout returns for a season finale on Thursday, November 19, 5–8 p.m., along The Embarcadero between Pier 14 and the Exploratorium. The free evening features artist vignettes and live music, free guided docent tours (departing Pier 14 at 5:30 and 6:30), professional holiday portraits at the Got Framed sculpture, and family-friendly activities.
