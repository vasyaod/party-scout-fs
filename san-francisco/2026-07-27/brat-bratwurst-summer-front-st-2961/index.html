Fri · 2026-07-31 · 4pm-9pm — Front Street Entertainment Zone, Financial District — Free

A free street pop-up landing one week before Charli xcx headlines SF's big August festival — a brat-pop street party with a lookalike contest and Bavarian beer-hall food, not just another club night.

Downtown SF Partnership and Noise Pop kick off their 2026 Fridays on Front Street series with Brat + Bratwurst Summer: brat-pop/indie-dance DJ sets from DJ Papa Xanny, JLittle and Bapari, a Charli xcx lookalike contest, wiener tossing, beer pong, and Bavarian beer-hall food from Schroeder's, Harrington's Bar & Grill and The Royal Exchange, Friday July 31, 4-9pm, free, all ages.
