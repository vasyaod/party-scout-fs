Sun · 2026-07-19 · 11am–2pm — Golden Gate Park, SF — Free

Free weekly swing dance with lessons behind the De Young — zero cost, outdoors, all skill levels welcome, running continuously since 1996

Lindy in the Park is a free outdoor Lindy Hop swing dance and lesson series on JFK Drive behind the de Young Museum in Golden Gate Park, every Sunday 11am to 2pm; all ages, rain cancels. Running since 1996, it is the longest-running swing dance venue in the Bay Area, founded by Chad Kubo and Ken Watanabe.
