A relaxed Friday-afternoon ride for elders, families and anyone wanting an easy, inclusive spin through the park, with adaptive bikes on hand.

All-ages, accessible group bike ride in Golden Gate Park hosted by SFBike, San Francisco Village and BORP Adaptive Sports. BORP supplies recumbent e-assist, side-by-side tandem and other adaptive bikes; bring your own if you prefer. The pace is slow and conversational and nobody gets dropped.
